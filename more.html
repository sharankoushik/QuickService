About QuickService

QuickService is a website that connects customers with trusted local service providers for their everyday home needs. Instead of searching for different workers separately, users can find and request services from one place.

Services We Provide

  • House Cleaning
  • Electrical Repairs
  • Carpentry
  • Vehicle Washing
  • Moving and Packing
  • Laundry Services

How It Works

  1. Create an account and log in to QuickService.
  2. Choose the home service you need.
  3. Enter your location and preferred time.
  4. View available local service providers.
  5. Compare ratings, price and experience.
  6. Book the service.
  7. Rate and review the provider after completion.

Platform Features

Service provider side: Providers can create profiles, select the services they offer, set their service area, receive booking requests, accept or reject jobs, and manage their schedules.

Admin side: The administrator can manage users and service providers, verify providers, monitor bookings, manage complaints, and view overall platform activity.
